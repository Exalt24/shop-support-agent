# Store policies (demo content for the dac-dev-store development store)

## Returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging. Start a return by replying with the order number and the item. Refunds go back to the original payment method after the item arrives and passes a quick check.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free for faulty or wrong items. For a change of mind, the customer pays the return postage.

## Shipping times
Orders are packed within 2 business days. Standard delivery then takes 3 to 7 business days. A tracking number is emailed as soon as the order ships.

## Order changes and cancellations
An order can be changed or cancelled while its status is unfulfilled. Once it has shipped it can no longer be changed, but it can be returned under the returns policy.

## Warranty
Electronics carry a 12 month warranty against manufacturing faults. Damage from drops, spills or misuse is not covered.

## Payment and invoices
Prices are shown in US dollars and include all taxes. Invoices are emailed after payment.
